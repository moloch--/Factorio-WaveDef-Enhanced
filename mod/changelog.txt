Version: 0.6.0
Date: 2026-10-11
  Features:
    - Add Tree coverage and Cliff density sliders and percentage fields under Starting area, ranging from 0% to 600%.
    - Preserve the scenario's original trees and cliffs at 100%; disable either feature at 0%.
    - Share the existing one-second preview debounce across terrain, resource, starting-area, and seed edits.
    - Report applied tree coverage and cliff density with /wde-status.
  Info:
    - Tree coverage adjusts forest size; cliff density fills gaps along the existing cliff contours without changing their elevation or spacing.
    - Terrain controls are independent of resource presets and starting-area mode. Active rounds remain unchanged until the next generation.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026-10-11
  Features:
    - Add the Wave Defense Enhanced scenario, which loads the game's original Wave Defense logic and supports automatic preview regeneration.
    - Regenerate the preview one second after the last setup edit, coalescing changes across controls and administrators.
    - Automatically apply edited seeds after the same delay; Enter and Shuffle still update immediately.
    - Keep invalid edits on screen, cancel obsolete refreshes after native rerolls, and preserve queued changes across save/load.
  Changes:
    - Launch the Enhanced scenario from the development launcher and debugger.
  Info:
    - Existing built-in Wave Defense saves retain manual preview updates because the original scenario exposes no regeneration interface.
    - Automatic regeneration only operates in the setup preview; changes during a round stay pending.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-10-11
  Features:
    - Add a Water amount slider and percentage field under Starting area, independent of resource presets and area mode.
    - Adjust water coverage on island and infinite battle maps from 0% to 600%, preserving native terrain at 100%.
    - Report the applied water setting with /wde-status.
  Bugfixes:
    - Preserve invalid starting-area and water edits when changing resource presets.
  Info:
    - Zero water removes lakes and the island ocean. Higher water settings preserve the immediate spawn area.
    - Water changes apply on the next preview regeneration or round restart.
